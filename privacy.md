Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 18, 2026
Our ER Discord bot helps members verify their Roblox accounts.
The bot may store Discord user IDs, Roblox user IDs, Roblox usernames, and verification status to link accounts and assign Discord roles.
We do not ask for Roblox passwords or sell personal information.
Information may be processed by Roblox, Discord, and the services used to host the bot.
To request account unlinking or deletion of verification data, contact the server administration team through our Discord server.
This policy may be updated as the bot changes.
